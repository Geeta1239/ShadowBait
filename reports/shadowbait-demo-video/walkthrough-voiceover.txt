Here is how ShadowBait turns a website into evidence.

First, we enter the separate demo-store URL, keeping the target experience independent from the analysis platform.

Next, ShadowBait opens a fresh browser and walks through the important routes, capturing screenshots, visible text, selectors, and state changes.

The pipeline classifies each observation and explains the possible customer impact.

In the evidence room, every finding stays connected to its original screenshot.

Finally, the interactive diff compares the pressured choice with a clearer, ethical alternative.

This is evidence before judgment.
